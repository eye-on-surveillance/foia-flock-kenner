This graph shows how lopsided the outside-agency searches of Kenner's cameras are: a handful of agencies account for a huge share of the volume, while thousands of others each show up only a few times.

## How the numbers were built

Every outside agency's searches are simply counted up and ranked from most to least. 

The ranked list is then grouped into bands for the treemap: the top 10 agencies, ranks 11 through 50, ranks 51 through 100, and everyone else — over 5,000 agencies — lumped into one "long tail" block. The long tail isn't broken out individually because thousands of slivers too thin to read wouldn't add any real information.

## Worked example

Houston TX PD alone ran 1,008,621 searches — 9.8% of all outside searches. Dallas TX PD ran 880,911 — 8.6%. Together, those two agencies (rank 1 and 2) add up to more than the next 48 agencies combined (ranks 3 through 50). That comparison is exactly what the "Top 10" block on the treemap is showing, at a larger scale.
